NACHOTAKIWA:
 Nakopya faili 6 zilizo hapa (backend_files/) kwenda EC2, halafu
 uzime service. HAKUNA API, HAKUNA SMS, HAKUNA FUNUO zaidi.

 Faili 6 ni hizi:
   app/modules/auth/routes.py
   app/modules/feedback/routes.py
   app/modules/matches/routes.py
   app/events/subscriber.py
   app/services/fcm.py

 NJIA YA 1 — Kama unatumia Termius / Putty / WinSCP:
   1. Fungua WinSCP (au SFTP yoyote). Ingia EC2 kwa
      username + password/key zako za kawaida.
   2. Pande kushoto (kompyuta yako), ingia kwenye folda
      ya project hii, fungua backend_files/.
   3. Pande kulia (EC2), ingia kwenye folda ya backend yako
      (ile yenye app/ na requirements.txt).
   4. Kopya faili zote 5 hapo juu — kila moja ichukue nafasi
      ya ile ya zamani (Overwrite = Ndiyo).
   5. Kwenye terminal ya EC2, endesha:
         sudo systemctl restart kv_backend
      (au jina la service yako kama si hilo; kama unatumia
      docker: sudo docker restart kv_backend)
   6. I METAYE. Hakuna kitu kingine.
